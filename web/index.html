Test4Test

Get your 12 testers for 14 days. Google Play asks new personal developer accounts for a closed test before production; Test4Test is the exchange where developers test each other's apps.

Get it on Google Play
  • Post your app with its Google Group and opt-in link, and other developers join your test.
  • Your round today: open the apps you test once a day from Test4Test. Owners see on how many of the last 14 days each tester opened their app, which is what Google looks at.
  • Comments on each test, to say you are in, ask, or link your own app so they test you back.

The free plan lists one app at a time and shows a small ad banner. The monthly subscription lists as many apps as you want, with no ads. Joining tests, commenting and browsing are always free. No reviews or ratings are exchanged.
