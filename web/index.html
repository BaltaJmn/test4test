Test4Test

Get the 12 testers Google Play asks for by testing other developers' apps.

Open this app in Test4Test

Test4Test is an Android app in closed testing. Three steps to get in, all with the same Google account:

  1. Join the Google Group Play only lets group members into the test.
  2. Accept the test on Google Play If Play shows an error page, step 1 is missing or used another account.
  3. Install Test4Test Then list your app and join a few others. Apps whose owners test others go first.

Keep the apps you join installed for 14 days: that is what Play counts, and Test4Test shows each owner who really has their app.
