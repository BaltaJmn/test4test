Test4Test: 12 testers for 14 days on Google Play

Google Play asks new personal developer accounts for a closed test before production. Test4Test is a free Android app where developers test each other's apps, so you can find your 12 testers.

Free on Google Play. Android only.

Get it on Google Play

How it works

  1. Set up your closed test. Create a Google Group, add it as the tester list of a closed test in Play Console and copy the opt-in link.
  2. List your app. In Test4Test, paste the Google Group link and the opt-in link. It takes about a minute.
  3. Join other tests. Open the group, open the opt-in link, opt in and install the app from Google Play. Four steps, in order.
  4. They join yours. The feed is a line in order of arrival, so everyone pushes the first apps until they reach 12. Developers who test others go ahead, and apps from subscribers go first, marked as featured.

What "your round today" does

A tester who installs an app and never opens it is not much of a test. Google's help page says a request can be held back for insufficient tester engagement, so Test4Test adds a daily habit.

Once a day, open the apps you test from your round. Each app owner sees on how many of the last 14 days every tester launched their app from Test4Test. Opening an app is not the same as using its features, so ask your testers to try them. A daily reminder keeps you on track, and the app tells you when a new tester joins your app; you can turn both off. You can also comment on each test, to say you are in or to link your own app so the owner tests you back.

Free and subscription

  • Free: one app listed at a time, with a small ad banner.
  • Monthly subscription: your apps first in line, unlimited apps and no ads. Cancel it anytime from Google Play.
  • Always free: joining tests, commenting and browsing.

What Test4Test does not do

It does not exchange reviews or ratings, and you should never offer rewards for installs or reviews. It does not promise approval. Google decides that, and the guides below link to Google's own help pages.

Questions

What is the 12 testers for 14 days rule?

New personal Google Play developer accounts must run a closed test with at least 12 testers, opted in continuously for at least 14 days, before they can apply for production access. Read the full guide.

Does Test4Test guarantee that Google will approve my app?

No. Nobody can promise that. Test4Test helps you find testers and shows on how many of the last 14 days each tester launched your app from Test4Test. Google makes the decision.

Is Test4Test free?

Yes. The free plan lists one app at a time and shows a small ad banner. The monthly subscription puts your apps first in line, lists as many as you want and removes the ads. Joining tests, commenting and browsing are always free.

Do testers have to write reviews?

No. No reviews or ratings are exchanged in Test4Test. Testers join your closed test and open your app. That is all.

My production access request was rejected. What now?

Check how many testers stayed opted in and whether they used the app, then fix what the app needs. The rejection guide has a checklist.
