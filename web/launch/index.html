How it works

  1. Email me your app link or package name, your raw screenshots (any phone, any size), a few lines about what the app does and the languages you want.
  2. Within 24 hours I tell you if the screenshots work or which ones need a retake, and send a payment link.
  3. In 3 working days you get the zip, or your listing live in Play Console with the launch kit.
  4. One round of changes is included: a headline, a color, the order of the images.

What it is not

I do not sell testers, installs, reviews or ratings, and I cannot promise that Google approves your app: Google decides that. Translations are not by native speakers. They are checked for meaning and length in every language, and if you have a native speaker for one, send their text and I will use it.

Questions

What screenshots should I send?

Plain captures from your phone or emulator, with demo data that looks real: no "test test" or empty lists. I crop the status bar and frame them. If a language changes what the screen shows, send that language's captures too; if not, one set is enough.

Who are you?

An indie developer from Spain with apps on Google Play and the App Store, and the person who builds Test4Test. These are the same tools I use for my own store pages.

How do I pay?

By card, through a payment link I send once your screenshots are confirmed. Prices in euros.
