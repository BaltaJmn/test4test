Test4Test Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026 · Espanol

Test4Test is a service that puts Android developers in touch with each other so they can help one another gather the testers Google Play requires before an app can be published. This policy explains exactly what data is processed, what for, and who can see it.

1. Data controller

Baltasar Jimenez Rincon.
Contact: baltax.75@gmail.com

Test4Test is a personal project, not a company. Writing to that address reaches the person who develops and administers it directly.

2. What data is processed

2.1 When you create an account

Sign-in is done with Google. Test4Test never sees or stores your Google password: Google performs the authentication and returns an already validated session. From that account the following is received and stored:

  • Your email address.
  • The public name on your Google account.
  • The address (URL) of your Google profile picture.
  • The internal identifier Google assigns to the account.

2.2 Which part of the profile is public

Your display name and profile picture are visible to anyone, including people without a Test4Test account. Your email address is not public and is not shown anywhere in the app.

If your Google account provides no name, the part of the email before the at sign is used as the public name. It can be changed later from your profile.

2.3 Content published by using the service

Everything below is public by design, because showing it is what the service is for:

  • The apps you list: name, Google Group link, Google Play link and testing opt-in link.
  • The apps you have joined as a tester.
  • The comments you write under any app, with their author and date.

2.4 Purchases

Test4Test offers a free plan with one app and a paid plan with unlimited apps. The purchase is made inside the Android app, through Google Play, and is handled by RevenueCat. Test4Test does not receive, see or store any payment data: no card numbers, no billing addresses, no cardholder name. All that arrives is a flag saying whether the paid plan is active.

2.5 What is not done

  • There is no analytics or behaviour measurement.
  • There is no advertising.
  • There are no tracking or third-party cookies.
  • No commercial profiling and no automated decision-making take place.
  • Data is never sold or handed to third parties for commercial purposes.

3. Storage on your device

So you are not asked to sign in on every visit, the session is kept in your browser local storage (localStorage) on the web version, and in the app private storage on Android. It is storage that is technically necessary for the service to work, it is not used for tracking, and that is why no cookie banner is shown. You can remove it by signing out or by clearing your browser or app data.

6. Moderation

Test4Test has administrators who can take down any app or comment that breaks the rules or is abusive. That is the only intervention on other people content that exists in the service.

7. How long data is kept

Data is kept for as long as the account exists. Deleting the account automatically and in cascade deletes the profile, the listed apps, the tester participations and the comments. A copy may remain for a limited period in the database backups, which are rotated and eventually disappear.

8. How to delete your account and your data

To delete your account, write to baltax.75@gmail.com from the same email address you use to sign in to Test4Test, saying that you want your account deleted.

Requests are handled within a maximum of 30 days. The account, the profile, the published apps, the tester participations and the comments are deleted. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

9. Your rights

Anyone can exercise their rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and portability by writing to baltax.75@gmail.com. A reply is given within one month at the most.

If the reply is not satisfactory, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency, www.aepd.es.

10. Children

Test4Test is aimed at people who develop apps and is not directed at children under 14. Data from children under that age is not knowingly collected. If such an account is found, it will be deleted.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the way the service works changes. The date in the header shows the latest version. If a change materially affects how data is processed, notice will be given inside the app.
