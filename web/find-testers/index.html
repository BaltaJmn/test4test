How to find 12 testers for a Google Play closed test

Finding the people is the hard part of the rule. Here are the ways that work, with the effort each one takes. Test4Test is our app, so weigh that one accordingly.

What you are looking for

Google's help page suggests using personal and professional networks, communities where potential users are active, and social media. It also suggests a diverse group that represents your audience, including different device types. Whoever they are, they need a Google Account, they have to opt in themselves and they must stay opted in for at least 14 days.

Fake accounts and paid testers

Do not create fake accounts or pay for installs. Google asks for real testers who use the app.

Friends, family and colleagues

This is the fastest start. Send them the four steps in one message: join the Google Group, open the opt-in link, opt in, install. Then ask them to open the app on different days. The weak point is follow-through. A friend who opts out on day 10 breaks the 14 continuous days.

Your own audience

If you have people who already want your app, such as newsletter readers or a small community, ask them directly. They match your real target audience, which helps when you describe it in the application.

Communities

Developer forums, chat servers and subreddits exist for this. Read each one's rules first and never offer money or rewards for installs or reviews. Offer something fair: you test theirs, they test yours.

Google Group swaps by hand

This is the classic swap. You post your group link, you join someone else's, and you keep track yourself. It works, but opting in is not the same as using the app, and a spreadsheet cannot tell you who opened what.

Keeping 12 testers for 14 days

Getting people to say yes is half the job. The other half is keeping them. Google advises telling testers they must stay opted in for at least 14 days and suggests a group chat for sharing feedback. Remind people on day 3, day 7 and day 12. Thank the ones who report something. Replace anyone who drops out early, and replace them fast, because every gap counts.

Our advice is to aim above 12, for example 15 people. Nobody can promise how many will stay.

A message you can copy

I am testing my Android app and Google needs 12 testers to stay opted in for 14 days. Could you: 1) join this Google Group, 2) open this link and opt in, 3) install the app, 4) open it every few days and tell me what feels wrong? Please do not opt out before two weeks have passed.

After you reach 12

Keep the test running and keep collecting feedback. You will need to summarize it in the application. When 14 days have passed, read the full guide before you apply.
