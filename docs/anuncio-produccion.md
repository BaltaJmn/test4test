    Hi all,

    Test4Test is now live on Google Play:
    https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.baltajmn.test4test

    It is an exchange for the closed testing rule (12 testers for 14 days): post your app,
    join other developers' tests, and they join yours.

    What helps you pass the production review:
    - "Your round today": open the apps you test once a day from Test4Test. Each owner sees
      on how many of the last 14 days every tester opened their app.
    - An optional evening reminder, so nobody drops off on day 9.
    - Comments on each test for real feedback.

    The free plan lists one app at a time. No reviews or ratings are exchanged, only real testing.
