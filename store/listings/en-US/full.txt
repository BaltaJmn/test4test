Google Play asks for 12 testers over 14 continuous days before a personal developer account can publish an app. Finding those people is the part nobody tells you about, and Google rejects the request when testers do not really use the app.

Test4Test is where Android developers find each other and get it done. You list your app, other developers join your closed test, and you test theirs. Real people on real devices, not a bot farm and not a spreadsheet in a chat group.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

- List your app with its Google Group and its opt-in link. That is all it takes.
- Browse the apps that still need testers. Developers who test others go first, and within that, the apps with the fewest testers.
- Join a test in four steps, in order: open the group, open the opt-in link, mark yourself in, install the app from Google Play.
- Leave a comment under an app to say you are in, ask a question, or link your own app so the developer can test you back.
- See who joined your test and whether they really have your app installed.
- Follow the 14-day streak: it starts the day you reach 12 testers, and the app tells you when it is done.

PROOF, NOT PROMISES

Pressing "I joined" proves nothing, and that is why most tester swaps fail. Test4Test checks on the tester's phone that your app is installed and that Google Play installed it, and shows it to you next to their name. It only looks at the apps they joined inside Test4Test, never at the rest of their phone.

WHY IT IS FAIR

Developers who test other apps rank above those who only post. Within each group, the apps with the fewest testers go first, so new ones do not sink. There is no way to buy a better position.

FREE AND SUBSCRIPTION

The free plan lists one app at a time, which is what most developers need, and shows a small ad banner. The subscription lists as many apps as you want and removes the ads; cancel it anytime from Google Play. Joining tests, commenting and browsing are always free.

PRIVACY

Your email address is never shown to anyone. Your name and profile picture are visible to other signed-in users, because that is how developers know who joined their test. Ads come from Google AdMob, and in Europe you choose whether they can be personalised. You can delete your account from the app at any time.
